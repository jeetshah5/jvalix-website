Frequently asked questions

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization structures your content so AI assistants and Google AI answers can cite your brand as the direct answer.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO ranks your pages in a list of links, while AEO makes your brand the answer itself. Jvalix does both together.

What are AI platform ads?

Paid placements inside AI answers and chats, like Google AI Mode, ChatGPT and Copilot. Jvalix plans, creates and manages them.

What are UGC ads?

Creator-style videos that look like real customer content, so they build trust and suit performance campaigns.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that does tasks for you, like replying to leads, answering customers or preparing reports, working around the clock with your tools and data.

What can generative AI do for my business?

It creates content, ad creatives, images, video and chatbots faster, and can power custom apps trained on your brand and data.
